# Arte original

`public/stadium.webp`: arte original criada com a ferramenta ImageGen integrada, convertida de PNG para WebP para o site. O PNG original foi mantido durante a produção. Sem marcas oficiais ou logotipos de torneios.

Prompt final:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset: ultrawide cinematic hero background for YAKASHIN, original Japanese football light novel reading platform. Dramatic premium hand-painted anime film still, a lone teenage Japanese male footballer with tousled dark hair seen from behind wearing an original deep navy Japan-inspired jersey number 10, standing on a lush dark green football pitch inside a vast Japanese stadium at NIGHT, looking towards the distant goal, very bright white-blue floodlights, fine rain and atmospheric blue haze, immense stands, gorgeous painterly cinematic detail. Player positioned in right third, cropped at shins, left half is dark atmospheric stadium negative space for text overlay. Deep midnight navy and electric blue palette, tiny warm gold light sparks. Wide landscape composition 16:9 or wider. No text, no branding, no watermark, no official team or tournament logos.

Tipografia: Barlow Condensed, DM Sans e Source Serif 4 via Google Fonts, com fontes de fallback locais.

Ícones: Lucide React.
